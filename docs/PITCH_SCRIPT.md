# Limit+ elevator pitch: "THE WAIT" (60 seconds)

## The idea

Waiting is the product. So the film is built out of time itself.

Three rules:
1. **Time is the only character.** A ticking clock is the whole soundtrack. Its tempo is the story: it speeds up into chaos, goes silent, slows as we dive, and stops dead at the hidden moment.
2. **One impossible camera move.** We pull back out of a single price line until it is a universe of waiting tokens, then dive in through three levels, each one a slower speed of time (one week, one auction, one moment). No hard cuts inside the move, only match cuts on shape.
3. **The totem.** Like the spinning top, the price tick hovers at the dashed line. We cut to black before it falls, because it does not matter which way it falls. You already got paid.

Colour discipline: obsidian black everywhere. Bone for type. Flame only for the candle and the dashed line. Amber only for money paid to you. Volt and iris only on the opening token field. If it is not one of those, it is not in frame.

Type: Archivo expanded black for the wordmark, Martian Mono for every number. Sentence case, no dashes.

## Structure (a palindrome)

The opening shot returns at the end, same tempo, same framing, but the dashed line now carries an amber "+ $6.20". The only thing that changed is that waiting pays.

## Voice over (read this)

Delivery: low, close to the mic, unhurried, almost a whisper. Pauses are written as (beat). Do not rush the silences, they are the loudest part.

```
0:00   Every stock is a token now.
0:04   Every limit order is someone waiting.
0:07   For free.

       (beat. two seconds of near silence. one candle.)

0:11   What if waiting... paid?

0:15   Limit plus.

       And market makers pay for your patience.

0:26   They bid in secret.

0:40   Friday's official close decides.

       (cut to black as the top is still spinning)

0:51   ...you already got paid.

0:55   Limit plus. Get paid while you wait.
```

About 80 words in 60 seconds. That is slow on purpose.

## Shot by shot

### ACT 0, THE WAIT (0:00 to 0:09)

**0:00 to 0:04, the single line.** Pure black. One NVDAx price chart, centred, tiny. A dashed flame line at $171. The price wiggles just above it. Sound: one clock tick per second, dry, close.
Camera: locked off. Nothing else moves. The stillness is the hook.

**0:04 to 0:09, the pull back.** Camera dollies back fast with an ease-in. The one chart becomes 8, then 80, then thousands of charts: every token, each with its own dashed line, each waiting. Tiles are volt and iris, the only colour in the film. Their ticks desync into a rising wall of clocks, tempo accelerating, a Shepard-style riser underneath that never resolves.
VO over the pull back: "Every stock is a token now. Every limit order is someone waiting. For free."

### THE HOLE (0:09 to 0:15)

**0:09 to 0:11, silence.** Every tick cuts at once. All the tiles desaturate to grey in a single frame. In the black, one flame candle lights, small, steady. Nothing else. This is the film's held breath.

**0:11 to 0:15, the turn.** VO: "What if waiting... paid?" On "paid" the field of grey dots rushes into the centre and assembles into the halftone **limit+** wordmark. The plus ignites flame. One huge low hit, felt more than heard (our own sub drop, nothing borrowed).

### ACT 1, THE DESCENT (0:15 to 0:37)

Three levels. Each has a small mono clock in the corner showing its own scale. Each runs slower than the last. We never cut: each level opens inside the one before it.

**LEVEL 1, one week (0:15 to 0:25).**
Match cut: the wordmark's plus becomes the centre of the candle ring. The ring sweeps through Monday to Friday in four seconds, corner clock reads `1 week`. The NVDAx price slider drags from $180 to $171. An amber counter rolls up to $6.20 and dots stream into a wallet.
VO: "Limit plus. Name your price. Deposit. And market makers pay for your patience."
Tick tempo: slows to 70 BPM.

**LEVEL 2, one auction (0:25 to 0:31).**
Match cut: the ring's flame arc becomes the edge of a glass card. We fall into the 3D room: sealed cards with the hatch pattern drop and hover face down, the dot field as the floor. Corner clock reads `1 auction`. Camera drifts between cards in slow motion.
VO: "They bid in secret."
Tick tempo: slows to 40 BPM.

**LEVEL 3, one moment (0:31 to 0:37).**
Match cut: the hatch pattern on a card becomes a single flame. Time almost stops: 8 times slow motion, the flame in extreme close-up, individual embers visible, corner clock reads `1 moment`. The ticks space out to one every two seconds.
VO: "And the auction ends... at a moment no one can predict."
**The candle goes out.** Hard cut to full black. Total silence for 0.7 seconds. The Shepard riser, which has been climbing since the first act, is pulled away in the same frame.

### THE KICK (0:37 to 0:40)

The drop. We shoot back up through all three levels in reverse at hyperspeed, rewind-whoosh sound, and on the way up the story resolves in streaks: the cards flip and stack into a price ladder, a glass plane slides across reading "Everyone pays 0.62%", the amber counter locks at $6.20. We land at Friday.

### ACT 2, THE TOTEM (0:40 to 0:51)

**0:40 to 0:46, two Fridays.** Screen splits vertically, a thin flame line as the seam. Left: "Friday close $165". The price falls through the dashed line and "5.84 NVDAx at your price" lands in a wallet. Right: "Friday close $185". The price stays above the line and "Your money back" lands. Both halves share one amber line across the seam: "+ the $6.20 you were paid".
VO: "Friday's official close decides. Stock at your price. Or your money back."

**0:46 to 0:51, the top.** The split collapses back into the single chart from the first shot, same framing, same dry tick. The price hovers right at the dashed line, wobbling. It is the spinning top. We hold on it.
VO: "Either way..."
Cut to black while it is still wobbling. In the black: "...you already got paid."

### CODA (0:51 to 1:00)

**0:51 to 0:55.** Black. The opening shot returns for one second, but the dashed line now carries an amber "+ $6.20". Same tick. The loop is closed.

**0:55 to 1:00.** The halftone wordmark on the dot field. Under it, in Archivo, sentence case: "Name your price. Get paid while you wait." Small line: "Live on Solana. Prices from Pyth. Example numbers shown." Second small line: "Company names and logos are trademarks of their owners. Not affiliated with or endorsed by them." Final hit, one second tail.

## Sound design (all original, nothing borrowed)

| Layer | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Clock tick | Dry mono tick, one sample | The whole film. Tempo map: 60 BPM, accelerating to chaos by 0:09, silent 0:09 to 0:11, 70 BPM, 40 BPM, 15 BPM, dead at 0:37, back at 60 BPM at 0:46 |
| Riser | Shepard tone, rising forever | Starts 0:04, never resolves, ripped away at the candle going out |
| Sub drop | 35 Hz sine with a short noise tail | 0:12 on the plus, 0:55 on the end card |
| Glass | Soft high chimes, one per card flip | 0:37 to 0:40 |
| Rewind | Filtered noise sweep, pitched up | The kick |
| Silence | True digital silence | 0:09 to 0:11 and the 0.7 seconds after the candle goes out |

## Edit notes

- 60 fps, delivered at 30 fps with motion blur for the fast moves (the pull back and the kick).
- Every move uses ease-in-out-expo. Nothing is linear.
- Exactly one "camera shake" in the film: on the sub drop at 0:12.
- No stock footage, no phone mock-ups, no logos except Solana and Pyth on the coda. Everything is rendered from the real design system.
- Subtitles burned in as lower-third captions, sentence case: they only repeat the VO at the three key lines ("What if waiting paid?", "They bid in secret.", "Either way, you already got paid.").

## How we make it

One HTML page, GSAP timeline, the app's own tokens, fonts and components (`HalftoneWordmark`, `AuctionStage`, `CandleRing`). Render deterministic frames by seeking the timeline frame by frame with Playwright (no real-time capture, so the 2-core machine cannot drop frames), then encode with ffmpeg and mux the synthesized sound design. You record the VO, we drop it in and trim the pauses.

## Delivery for LinkedIn and X

Both feeds autoplay muted, so the film must work with the sound off and be even better with it on.

**Formats**
- Master: 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350, 30 fps, H.264, AAC audio, under 100 MB, 60 seconds. This takes the most feed space on mobile on both platforms and needs no cropping.
- Secondary: 16:9, 1920 x 1080, same cut, for the website, the pinned repo and desktop embeds.
- Check each platform's current limits before posting: they change. 60 seconds is well inside both.

**Changes to the cut for 4:5**
- Safe zones: keep everything important inside the middle 80%. Top 8% and bottom 15% are reserved for platform UI and the captions.
- The 0:40 split screen splits top and bottom instead of left and right. The seam is still the thin flame line.
- The wordmark is stacked by letter pairs only if needed: first try full width, it already fits at this ratio.
- The token field in Act 0 is a vertical pull back, so the first frame is one tiny chart in the middle of a tall black frame.

**Muted-first captions (burned in, always on)**
- Caption every VO line, sentence case, Archivo semibold, bone on a faint obsidian bar. Two lines max.
- The two seconds of silence at 0:09 and the 0.7 seconds after the candle goes out get an on-screen line so they read as intent, not a glitch: "Every limit order is someone waiting." then "What if waiting paid?"
- Hook inside 3 seconds: the single dashed line with the clock tick, plus the caption "You wait for free."
